#About Me

My name is Eva Stewart, and I am a mechanical engineering student with a strong interest in motorsports, mechanical design, and performance engineering. My interest in engineering began at a young age when I had the opportunity to visit Boeing with my grandfather during a Bring Your Kid to Work Day. Seeing aircraft components being manufactured and assembled introduced me to the precision, problem-solving, and teamwork required in engineering. One activity in particular, designing a small wing to withstand applied force, sparked my interest in understanding how engineering principles can be used to solve real-world problems.

As I continued through school, I initially planned to pursue aerospace engineering. However, my growing interest in Formula One and motorsports led me to explore another side of engineering. I became fascinated by how engineers use aerodynamics, materials, mechanical systems, and data to improve vehicle performance. I realized that mechanical engineering would provide me with a broad technical foundation while allowing me to pursue my interest in motorsports. 

As a mechanical engineering student, I am developing into an engineer who values precision, problem-solving, and continuous improvement. I am particularly interested in the design and development of high-performance mechanical systems. I enjoy understanding how and why a system works and then using that knowledge to develop practical solutions.

My goal is to build a career in motorsports engineering, particularly in race car design and development. I understand that reaching this goal will require more than technical knowledge; it will require the ability to communicate effectively, work as part of a team, learn from failure, and defend engineering decisions using evidence and sound reasoning. Through my education and engineering experiences, I am working to develop those skills and become an engineer capable of contributing meaningful solutions to complex mechanical problems.
