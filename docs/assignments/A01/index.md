Task A: Portfolio Analysis

  The first engineering portfolio I reviewed was [TJ Watson's](https://www.tjwatson.net/). Unlike portfolios hosted on GitHub, Watson’s portfolio uses a personal website, which gives it a different overall structure and presentation. The website is well organized and easy to navigate, with clearly labeled sections for his projects, work experience, education, and other information.
  
  One aspect I found less effective was the projects section. If a visitor wants more details about his projects or to understand his thought process, they are directed to his LinkedIn profile for additional information. In contrast, the work experience section provides much more detail. Watson describes his internships and previous positions, including the companies he worked for and the responsibilities and skills he gained from each experience. Overall, I think the portfolio does a good job presenting his engineering background in a professional and organized way, but the projects section could provide more technical information directly on the website. 

  The second engineering portfolio I reviewed was [Frederick Wachter's](https://fwachter.github.io/). His portfolio is hosted through GitHub and has a more unique layout compared to a traditional portfolio website. When first opening the site, the navigation is not immediately obvious, which can make it somewhat difficult to find the specific information. Near the bottom of the page, there are navigation dots that allow you to move between the different sections of the portfolio. Once I figured out how the navigation worked, it became much easier to explore his engineering experience, project experience, and other information.
  
  One aspect I liked about Wachter’s portfolio was the amount of detail he provides about his engineering and project experience. For each project, he explains his role, the work he completed, and what he was responsible for. He also includes links to additional information for anyone interested in learning more about a specific project. Overall, I think his portfolio does a good job of demonstrating his technical experience and giving potential employers a better understanding of the skills he has developed through his projects.
